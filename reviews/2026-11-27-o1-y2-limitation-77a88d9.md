## The impossibility the builder and lead found is real

Verified independently at `6c5c9fe`, not taken from their messages:

- `BotPlayer.java:77` — `private BotPlayer(ServerPlayer player)`; the only other
  construction site is `spawn`, which needs a real `MinecraftServer` and a
  `BotServerPlayer`.
- `BotPlayer.java:55` — `private final ServerPlayer player`; no setter; `BotPlayer`
  itself is not final but its `player` cannot be replaced.
- `BotPlayer.java:1286` `findBlocks` and `:1313` `findEntities` are total over
  request data: an unknown id never matches, `getBlockState` does not throw,
  `getEntities` returns a list, and a malformed body is a 400 before the handler.

So no test in this repo can construct the object `handleBotAction` calls
`findBlocks` on, and no request can make either provider throw. Both halves of the
original 5.6 were unachievable, not merely inconvenient.

**My admission condition is what killed Y.1.** I required that reverting a
handler's send line to `Map.of(...)` must redden the new test. Under an extracted
`Executor` that mutant stays green, because a test reaching the switch body hits a
stub `BotPlayer` — a sibling of the real call. The check was correct; the candidate
failed it. 77 edits would have bought no confidence.
