## Design (accepted at `7574de8`, unchanged since)

- **Strip-before-parse channel over a second planner call.** One deterministic parse; the
  script travels in a channel `_strip_codefence`'s successor removes before `json.loads`, so
  `response_raw` and `parsed` agree and the DPO-trained field is clean by construction. A
  second call would double planning latency and cost per goal for the same guarantee.
- **Atomicity and one-driver stated as guarantees, not primitives** — lock, atomic swap, or
  single-object replacement all satisfy them, so the spec does not pre-commit the implementation.
- **Hold-release is coherent because the bind replaces the rival** (`agent.py:3185`); the
  hand-written case is stated separately, where the bind is refused and the hold stands.
- **The two remaining "resumes" hits** (`spec:322-323`, `proposal:26`) are the ordinary
  hold lifecycle (M3-1g), not the bind-replaces path — correct as written, and correctly
  flagged rather than silently left.
