## 3. F1b states the measured bound — CONFIRMED for `say`, WRONG for `send`

The added paragraph:

    One known gap bounds that guarantee: the window lives on the sandbox
    (`engine.js:251`, documented at `engine.js:399-406`), so a sandbox recycle
    after a `memory_exceeded`, a guard eviction or a wasm trap restarts it empty.
    The honest bound is therefore 30 per rolling minute plus up to 30 more per
    recycle. An ordinary script throw does not recycle the sandbox
    (`recycle.js:45-49`).

Every number and citation is mine and correct:

- 30 per rolling 60 s for `say`, confirmed in the amendment to
  `reviews/2026-11-20-script-say-send-delivery-8e1b3a5.md` (fdd982f).
- The recycle restarting the window empty: `engine.js:251` creates `#botState`
  per Sandbox, `worker.js:43` holds one per process, and a recycle replaces the
  process. Measured 30 -> 60 in the same rolling minute, 30 x (N+1) for N
  recycles.
- `recycle.js:45-49`: `mustRecycle` returns false for every kind in
  `SCRIPT_RAISED`, so an ordinary throw does not reset it.

**F1b-1 — required.** The paragraph reads "30 per rolling minute plus up to 30
more per recycle", but the sentence it annotates names two budgets: `api.say`
30/min and `api.send` 20/min. For `send` the honest bound is **20 per rolling
minute plus up to 20 more per recycle**. As written, the spec grants `send` a
recycle allowance 50% larger than its own cap. The fix is a clause, e.g. "30
per rolling minute for `say` and 20 for `send`, plus up to the same amount again
per recycle". Worth doing now because this is the paragraph archive will make
authoritative.
