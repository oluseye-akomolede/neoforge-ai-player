## On my earlier finding

My N-1 said the anchor was entirely by construction and might be
unfalsifiable. **That was too strong, and the builder was right to push back.**
The assertion is falsifiable, and I demonstrated it: an accessor that charges
`accept` reds exactly the two tests the commit names. My narrower and still
true point stands — the test pins the *construction* of `look` (it cannot see
the binding), not the *live worker path*, which is where the charge would have
to be introduced. That is a scope note, not a defect, and the commit is honest
about it.

## The one thing I could not certify

The spec sentence at `f37d337` says "the binding reports `host_calls` per
tick". That is true of `binding.js` (`state.hostCalls`, reported at `:507`),
but the sandbox tests read `sandbox.tickState.hostCalls`, a field the binding
path does not write. The two are the same number only because `look` never
calls `accept`. Stated here so nobody later reads the test as having exercised
`binding.js`'s counter.
