## P-1 (non-blocking): the PLAN preview hides the shape it exists to reveal

The PLAN site previews **`_strip_codefence(...)` output**, not the raw reply. `_strip_codefence`
(`:1266`) removes a leading fence line and a trailing fence. So a fenced, unparseable reply previews
as its interior:

```
model sent : '```json\n{broken\n```'
preview    : '{broken'
```

The docstring's stated purpose is to show "the shape of what actually came back (a think tag, prose
before the fence, a trailing comma)". For the fence case — plausibly the most common malformed
shape — the fence is exactly what is removed. The SCRIPT site has no such problem, because it
previews `answer_text(body)` unstripped; think tags and prose both survive there. I verified the
asymmetry on a real `answer_text` call.

**Not a blocker.** The preview still shows the payload, and on a fence-stripped reply the *content*
is the actionable part. It is a diagnostic that is slightly weaker than advertised on one of its two
sites, and it costs nothing to fix later by previewing the pre-strip string at `:496`. Worth noting
because the lead's next step is to read these lines during the 2a.9 trial: if a fenced reply fails to
parse, the log will show the interior rather than the fence, and that should not be misread as "the
model sent no fence".
